Jirayos Channel Stats

Jirayos Channel Stats is a personal, read-only tool that lets its owner view the statistics, analytics and estimated revenue of their own YouTube channel inside the Claude Desktop app.

It uses the YouTube Data API v3 and the YouTube Analytics API with read-only permissions. It cannot upload, edit or delete anything on YouTube. It runs only on the owner's own computer and is not offered as a public service.

What it shows

  • Channel overview: subscribers, views, watch time and estimated revenue for a chosen period
  • Per-video performance, daily and monthly trends
  • Audience breakdowns such as country, traffic source and device
